KEYBOARD OMARCHY BINDINGS, REPRODUCED

SUPER belongs to your operating system while a browser is open, so the bench reads ALT as SUPER. You can also latch the [ SUPER ] button on screen and press the plain keys. Some chords (ALT+TAB, ALT+digits in a few browsers) are still taken by the OS or the browser; the buttons below the desk do the same job.
